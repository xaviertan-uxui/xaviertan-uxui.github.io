About Me

Hi, I'm Xavier, a UI/UX designer based in Singapore with a background in media and design. I design clear, user-centric digital experiences, specialising in responsive web and mobile interfaces that improve usability. I also explore interactive and game design workflows using Unity and 3D tools, which shape my approach to interaction design and systems thinking.

My Work Approach

I design with a balance between functionality and aesthetics. A product should be intuitive and easy to use, while still feeling polished and visually engaging.

My process is grounded in user research. Understanding user needs and behaviours helps shape the direction of a product early on, ensuring that design decisions are purposeful and aligned with real use cases.

From there, I iterate through wireframes and prototypes to refine the experience before moving into final design.
