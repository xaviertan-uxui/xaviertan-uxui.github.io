BlueTel Campaigns

Campaign landing pages and digital assets designed to increase visibility and awareness of BlueTel's services.

This project focused on designing marketing campaigns for BlueTel, including landing pages and supporting digital assets such as EDMs and promotional content.

Role Web Designer, Marketing
Year 2025
Timeline 4 months
Link https://www.bluetel.net.sg/SubseaTerrestrial

Key Screens / Visuals

UX Highlights

10G / 100G Campaign design direction

Designed a side-by-side comparison layout to clearly communicate differences between the two services. A supporting map visualization was included to improve scanning and help users quickly understand availability.

Subsea-Terrestrial Campaign design direction

Focused on improving awareness of a new service through a clear above-the-fold CTA linking to a datasheet. An infographic diagram was used to simplify complex technical information into an easily digestible visual format.
