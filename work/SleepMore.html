SleepMore

A mobile app designed to help users improve sleep habits through tracking and gamification.

This project explores how a mobile app can encourage better sleep behaviour among students by combining sleep tracking with motivational game mechanics.

Role UX Research, UX/UI Designer
Year 2022
Timeline 3 months
Link https://figmashort.link/Rjit4R

Key Screens / Visuals

UX Highlights

Sleep Tracking & Insights

Designed a dashboard that summarises sleep patterns and highlights potential factors affecting sleep, helping users better understand their habits.

Gamified Incentive System

Introduced a reward system where users earn in-app currency through consistent sleep, encouraging healthier behaviour through customisation and progression.
